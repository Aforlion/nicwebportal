# 3. CORE PROGRAMME PATHWAY

## 3.1 Nursing Assistant Certification Pathway

The principal structured pathway discussed in this knowledge base is:

**Level 1 Fundamentals → Level 2 Specialisation → Clinical Internship → Nursing Assistant Certification**

The pathway combines:

1. Foundational caregiver education
2. Specialised caregiver education
3. Supervised hands-on practical experience
4. Professional assessment and certification
5. Credential verification

---

# 4. LEVEL 1 – FUNDAMENTALS OF PROFESSIONAL CAREGIVING

## Programme Name

**Level 1 – Fundamentals of Professional Caregiving**

## Duration

**120 learning hours**

## Delivery

**Online**

## Learning Format

**Self-paced**

## Fee

**₦200,000**

## Practical Component

The Level 1 programme is not purely theoretical.

Practical caregiving skills, demonstrations and competency-related assessment are incorporated into the training.

All academic training and applicable assessments can be completed online.

## Physical Attendance

Physical attendance is not required for the Level 1 academic component.

The physical, supervised practical experience occurs through the clinical internship component of the pathway.

## Additional Fees

There are no separate compulsory fees for:

* Practical training
* Assessments
* Examinations
* Certificate issuance

# 6. CLINICAL INTERNSHIP

## Purpose

The clinical internship provides supervised, hands-on practical experience in an appropriate healthcare or caregiving environment.

It is an important practical component of the Nursing Assistant Certification pathway.

## Internship Cost

**₦150,000–₦200,000**

The exact cost may depend on the placement arrangement.

## Abuja Placement

Candidates in Abuja can book their clinical internship through NIC.

Candidates do not necessarily have to independently source their own internship facility in Abuja.

## Placement Environment

Internship takes place through an appropriate:

* Accredited healthcare facility
* NIC partner facility
* Caregiving facility
* Other approved practical-training environment

## Exact Internship Hours

**Do not invent a fixed number of internship hours.**

If asked for exact hours, the agent should respond:

> "The clinical internship provides supervised, hands-on practical experience. The exact placement duration and practical hours are confirmed as part of the internship arrangement."

## Exact Internship Duration

**Do not invent a fixed number of weeks or months.**

The exact duration depends on the approved internship arrangement.

## Internship Documentation

Completed internship experience is formally documented and verified as part of the candidate's professional record.

## Internship Facility Examples

Do not provide names of specific facilities unless those facilities have been explicitly approved for public disclosure by NIC.

If asked:

> "NIC arranges clinical internship through appropriate accredited healthcare or partner facilities. Specific placement options are confirmed with the candidate during internship booking."

# 16. INTERNATIONAL RECOGNITION

This section is extremely important.

## Recognition vs Verification

The agent must distinguish between:

**Credential verification**

and

**International recognition/equivalence**

NIC can provide verifiable credentials.

However, verification does not automatically mean that a foreign employer, regulator, immigration authority or educational institution will recognise the qualification for a particular purpose.

## U.S. Employment

NIC training may contribute to a professional caregiver profile.

However, employment decisions are made by employers.

## U.S. EB-3

NIC does **not**:

* Guarantee EB-3 sponsorship
* Guarantee an EB-3 job
* Guarantee visa approval
* Determine immigration eligibility
* Act as a U.S. immigration authority

EB-3 eligibility depends on the specific employment position, employer, immigration requirements and the individual's circumstances.

## U.S. CNA Equivalence

Do not describe the NIC Nursing Assistant Certificate as automatically equivalent to a U.S. state-issued CNA licence.

Correct response:

> "The NIC Nursing Assistant Certificate is an NIC professional qualification. It should not be represented as automatically equivalent to a U.S. state-issued CNA licence. Acceptance depends on the requirements of the specific employer, state or institution."

## Ireland / QQI

Do not state that an NIC qualification is automatically equivalent to **QQI Level 5**.

Correct response:

> "NIC does not represent its qualification as automatically equivalent to QQI Level 5. Comparability or acceptance is determined by the relevant employer, institution or assessment authority."

## Europe

Do not claim blanket recognition throughout Europe.

Correct response:

> "Acceptance of a foreign caregiver qualification varies by country, employer and role. Applicants should confirm the requirements of the specific destination and employer."

# 20. COMMON QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS

## "How much is the full Nursing Assistant programme?"

> "For the Level 1 → Geriatrics & Gerontology → Clinical Internship pathway, the estimated total is ₦505,000–₦555,000, including ₦5,000 NIC membership. There are no separate compulsory fees for practicals, assessments or certificate issuance."

## "Is it online?"

> "Yes. The academic components are online and self-paced. The clinical internship is the physical, supervised practical component."

## "Do I need to come to Abuja?"

> "Not for the online academic training or assessments. If you choose to undertake your clinical internship in Abuja, you can book the internship through NIC."

## "How long is the internship?"

> "The internship is a supervised practical component of the Nursing Assistant pathway. The exact duration and practical hours are confirmed as part of the internship placement arrangement."

## "Can you show me a certificate sample?"

> "NIC currently does not provide specimen certificates or transcripts to prospective applicants. Official documents are issued after successful completion of the applicable programme requirements."

## "Is this certificate recognised in America?"

> "NIC provides a verifiable professional qualification. However, acceptance in the United States depends on the specific employer, state, institution or regulatory requirement. We do not claim automatic U.S. equivalence."

## "Can this get me an EB-3 visa?"

> "NIC does not guarantee EB-3 sponsorship or visa approval. EB-3 employment and immigration eligibility depend on the U.S. employer and applicable U.S. immigration requirements."

## "Is it equivalent to QQI Level 5?"

> "NIC does not claim automatic equivalence to QQI Level 5. Comparability or acceptance must be determined by the relevant employer, institution or assessment authority."

## "Can I study from another country?"

> "Yes. The academic components are online and self-paced. You can complete them remotely and arrange your clinical internship in Nigeria when you are ready."
