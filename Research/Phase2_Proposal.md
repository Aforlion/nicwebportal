# National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria)

# Institutional Training, Accreditation & NCNA Certification Framework

## Product Specification

## Project Vision

The National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria) is expanding its digital platform from a Learning Management System (LMS) into a comprehensive **Learning, Accreditation, Membership, and Professional Certification Platform**.

NIC Nigeria serves as the national professional body responsible for setting caregiver training standards, accrediting training institutions, approving curricula, verifying internships, and issuing the **NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA)** credential.

The platform shall support three primary user groups:

* Individual Members (Students)
* Accredited Training Partners
* NIC Nigeria Administrators

---

# 1. Accredited Training Partners

Introduce a new organization type:

**Accredited Training Partner**

These organizations are institutions approved by NIC Nigeria to train caregiver students.

Each Accredited Training Partner will have:

* Institution Profile
* Accreditation Status
* Unique Institution Code
* Approved Instructors
* Student Management Dashboard
* Curriculum Status
* Annual Accreditation Record

---

# 2. Accreditation Framework

Every institution must complete NIC Nigeria's accreditation process before enrolling students.

## Level 1 — NIC Nigeria Registered Training Partner

Requirements

* Business registration
* Physical training location
* Institutional profile
* Acceptance of NIC Nigeria policies

Badge

🟦 NIC Nigeria Registered Training Partner

---

## Level 2 — NIC Nigeria Accredited Training Partner

Requirements

* Approved curriculum (NIC curriculum or NIC-approved institutional curriculum)
* At least one approved instructor
* Successful compliance review

Badge

🟩 NIC Nigeria Accredited Training Partner

---

## Level 3 — NIC Nigeria Centre of Excellence

Requirements

* Consistently high training standards
* Positive student outcomes
* Successful annual audits
* Continued compliance

Badge

🟨 NIC Nigeria Centre of Excellence

---

Accreditation is valid for **one year** and must be renewed annually.

The system should automatically notify institutions before accreditation expires.

Institution profiles throughout the portal should display the appropriate accreditation badge.

---

# 3. Institution Registration & Accreditation Fee

To become an Accredited Training Partner, an institution shall pay a **one-time registration and accreditation fee of ₦100,000**.

This fee covers:

* Institutional registration
* Initial accreditation
* Institution profile creation
* Institution dashboard activation
* Unique Institution Code

Subsequent years require **annual accreditation renewal** according to NIC Nigeria's renewal policy.

---

# 4. Unique Institution Code

Every accredited institution shall automatically receive a permanent Institution Code.

Example

NIC-ABC-2026

The Institution Code uniquely identifies the training partner within the NIC Nigeria ecosystem.

Students registering through an institution must provide this code during registration.

---

# 5. Student Registration

During registration, every applicant shall answer:

**"Are you registering through an NIC Nigeria Accredited Training Partner?"**

Options

* Yes
* No

If Yes

The student enters the Institution Code.

The system validates the code.

Once validated

* Student is linked to the institution.
* Student appears automatically in the institution dashboard.
* The institution receives credit for the student's registration.

Students registering independently simply continue without entering an Institution Code.

---

# 6. Membership Model

Every trainee, regardless of where they receive classroom instruction, must become an NIC Nigeria member.

Each student is responsible for paying the applicable NIC Nigeria membership fee during registration.

Membership gives access to:

* Student portal
* Learning records
* Professional profile
* Certification tracking
* Internship tracking
* Certificate verification
* Digital badges

Membership is separate from course fees.

---

# 7. Course Billing Model

There are two approved training models.

## Option A — Institution Uses NIC Nigeria Curriculum

The institution delivers NIC Nigeria's approved curriculum.

Students register with NIC Nigeria and:

* Pay NIC membership fees.
* Enrol in NIC courses.
* Pay the applicable NIC course fees.

When registering for a course, the student enters the Institution Code.

The system automatically recognizes the Accredited Training Partner and applies the partner arrangement.

NIC Nigeria records the institution as the referring training partner and automatically allocates the agreed partner commission or revenue share to that institution.

This process should be fully automated.

---

## Option B — Institution Uses Its Own Approved Curriculum

The institution delivers its own curriculum after obtaining approval from NIC Nigeria.

Students still:

* Register with NIC Nigeria.
* Pay NIC membership fees.

However,

No NIC course fee is charged because the institution is delivering its own approved curriculum.

NIC Nigeria remains responsible for:

* Student records
* Internship verification
* Professional certification
* NCNA issuance

---

# 8. Instructor Approval

Every Accredited Training Partner must maintain at least one Approved Instructor.

To qualify, an instructor must successfully complete:

* NIC Nigeria Fundamental Caregiving Course
* At least one NIC Nigeria Advanced Specialty Course

Only Approved Instructors may supervise NIC Nigeria accredited training programmes.

Institutions without an approved instructor cannot maintain active accreditation.

---

# 9. Curriculum Approval

Institutions may choose one of two approaches.

## Adopt NIC Nigeria Curriculum

Curriculum approval is automatic.

The institution becomes eligible for the NIC curriculum partner arrangement.

---

## Submit Institutional Curriculum

Institution uploads:

* Curriculum
* Modules
* Learning outcomes
* Assessment methods
* Programme duration

NIC Nigeria reviews the submission.

Possible outcomes

* Approved
* Revision Required
* Rejected

Approved institutions receive a Curriculum Approval Certificate.

---

Modules include:

Students

Curriculum

Accreditation

Partner Earnings

# 11. Professional Certification Pathway

NIC Nigeria distinguishes between **course completion** and **professional certification**.

Course certificates confirm successful learning.

Professional certification confirms competence to practise.

The pathway is:

Stage 1

Complete NIC Nigeria Fundamental Caregiving Course

↓

Receive

Fundamental Course Certificate

↓

Stage 2

Complete one NIC Nigeria Advanced Specialty Course

↓

Receive

Advanced Specialty Certificate

↓

Stage 3

Complete a minimum three-month supervised internship

↓

Internship verified by NIC Nigeria

↓

Receive

Internship Verification Certificate

↓

Stage 4

System verifies

✓ Fundamental completed

✓ Advanced Specialty completed

✓ Internship verified

↓

Status

Eligible for NCNA

↓

Stage 5

NIC Nigeria awards

**NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA)**

Only NIC Nigeria may issue this credential.

---

# 12. Certification Engine

The system shall automatically determine each student's certification status.

Statuses include:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* Awaiting Internship
* Internship Under Review
* Eligible for NCNA
* NCNA Certified

Eligibility requires:

* Fundamental Course completed
* Advanced Specialty Course completed
* Internship successfully verified

---

# 13. Certificate Types

The platform shall generate:

1. Fundamental Course Certificate

2. Advanced Specialty Certificate

3. Internship Verification Certificate

4. NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA) Certificate

5. Accredited Training Partner Certificate

6. Curriculum Approval Certificate

---

# 14. Digital Badges

Students earn professional digital badges for:

* Fundamental Graduate
* Advanced Specialty Graduate
* Internship Verified
* NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (Gold)

Badges appear on:

* Student Profile
* Public Verification Profile
* Downloadable Badge Assets

---

# 15. Certificate Design Standards

## Fundamental Course Certificate

* Academic design
* Silver and blue accents
* Clean professional layout

---

## Advanced Specialty Certificate

* Academic design
* Silver and blue accents
* Clearly identifies specialization

---

## Internship Verification Certificate

* Formal verification style
* Grey and green accents
* Official verification appearance

---

## NCNA Certificate

The NCNA Certificate is NIC Nigeria's flagship professional credential.

Design specifications:

* Premium cream background
* Gold ornamental border
* Embossed NIC Nigeria seal
* Large certificate number
* QR verification code
* Multiple authorized signatures
* Elegant typography
* High-security professional layout

Certificate wording shall include:

**"Issued by the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria) as the Certification Authority."**

---

# 16. Certificate Verification

Every certificate shall include:

* Certificate Number
* QR Code
* Verification URL
* Student Name
* Qualification
* Date Issued
* Institution (where applicable)

Public verification endpoint:

/verify/{certificate_number}

Employers and other stakeholders should be able to instantly verify the authenticity of any NIC Nigeria-issued credential.

---

# 17. System Architecture

The NIC Nigeria Portal shall operate as four integrated systems within a unified platform:

1. Membership Management System

   * Member registration
   * Membership records
   * Professional profiles

2. Learning Management System (LMS)

   * Courses
   * Assessments
   * Learning resources

3. Accreditation Management System

   * Institutional accreditation
   * Instructor approval
   * Curriculum approval
   * Annual accreditation renewal

4. Professional Certification System

   * Internship verification
   * Certification eligibility engine
   * NCNA certification
   * Digital badges
   * Certificate verification

The National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria) remains the sole Certification Authority responsible for awarding the **NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA)** credential, ensuring that every certified professional has successfully completed standardized theoretical training, advanced specialization, and verified practical experience in accordance with NIC Nigeria's professional standards.
