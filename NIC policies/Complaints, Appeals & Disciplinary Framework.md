# National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria)

# Regulatory Framework Manual

## Part XII — Complaints, Appeals & Disciplinary Framework

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Complaints, Appeals & Disciplinary Framework establishes the procedures for receiving, investigating, resolving, and determining complaints, appeals, and disciplinary matters within the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria).

The Framework ensures that all matters are handled fairly, consistently, transparently, and in accordance with the principles of natural justice.

Its purpose is to protect the public, maintain confidence in the caregiving profession, preserve the integrity of NIC Nigeria, and safeguard the rights of all persons involved.

---

# 2. Objectives

This Framework seeks to:

* Provide a transparent complaint management process.
* Ensure fairness to complainants and respondents.
* Promote accountability and professional responsibility.
* Protect learners, caregivers, care recipients, and the public.
* Resolve disputes promptly and impartially.
* Establish a consistent disciplinary process.
* Provide an effective appeal mechanism.

---

# 3. Scope

This Framework applies to:

* Members of NIC Nigeria.
* Student Members.
* Certified Professionals.
* Approved Instructors.
* Accredited Training Partners.
* Accredited Care Facilities.
* Accredited Care Agencies.
* Approved Internship Providers.
* Applicants for membership, accreditation, or certification.

---

# 4. Guiding Principles

All proceedings shall be guided by the following principles:

* Fairness.
* Impartiality.
* Confidentiality.
* Transparency.
* Timeliness.
* Accountability.
* Proportionality.
* Respect for the rights of all parties.

No person shall be subjected to disciplinary action without being given an opportunity to respond to the allegations.

---

# 5. Complaints

A complaint may be submitted by:

* A member of the public.
* A care recipient or family representative.
* A member of NIC Nigeria.
* An Accredited Organisation.
* A government agency.
* An employer.
* The Governing Council.
* Any person with a legitimate interest in the matter.

Complaints may be submitted through the NIC Nigeria Portal or by any other approved method.

---

# 6. Matters That May Be Reported

Complaints may relate to:

* Professional misconduct.
* Ethical violations.
* Abuse or neglect.
* Academic malpractice.
* Fraud or misrepresentation.
* Unsafe caregiving practices.
* Accreditation breaches.
* Instructor misconduct.
* Internship irregularities.
* Misuse of NIC Nigeria credentials.
* Non-compliance with Institute policies.

---

# 7. Preliminary Assessment

Upon receipt of a complaint, NIC Nigeria shall conduct a preliminary assessment to determine:

* Whether the matter falls within the Institute's jurisdiction.
* Whether sufficient information has been provided.
* Whether immediate action is required to protect the public.
* Whether the matter should proceed to formal investigation.

Complaints outside the Institute's jurisdiction may be referred to the appropriate authority.

---

# 8. Investigation

Where appropriate, NIC Nigeria shall appoint an Investigation Officer or Investigation Panel.

The investigation may include:

* Review of documents.
* Interviews.
* Site visits.
* Digital records.
* Statements from relevant parties.
* Expert opinion where necessary.

Investigations shall be conducted impartially and without undue delay.

---

# 9. Interim Measures

Where necessary to protect learners, care recipients, or the public, NIC Nigeria may impose temporary measures during an investigation, including:

* Temporary suspension of accreditation.
* Temporary suspension of instructor approval.
* Temporary suspension of professional certification.
* Temporary suspension of membership.
* Restrictions on specified activities.

Interim measures are precautionary and do not constitute a finding of guilt.

---

# 10. Disciplinary Hearing

Where an investigation indicates that disciplinary action may be warranted, the matter shall be referred to a Disciplinary Panel.

The respondent shall have the right to:

* Receive written notice of the allegations.
* Review relevant evidence.
* Submit a written response.
* Present evidence.
* Call witnesses where appropriate.
* Be accompanied by a representative, if permitted under NIC Nigeria procedures.

The Disciplinary Panel shall consider all relevant evidence before reaching a decision.

---

# 11. Decisions

Following a disciplinary hearing, the Panel may determine that:

* No breach has occurred.
* Advice or guidance should be issued.
* Corrective action is required.
* Misconduct has been established.
* Serious misconduct has been established.

Decisions shall be communicated in writing with reasons.

---

# 12. Disciplinary Sanctions

Where misconduct is established, NIC Nigeria may impose one or more of the following sanctions:

* Advisory Letter.
* Formal Warning.
* Mandatory Training or Retraining.
* Corrective Action Plan.
* Probation.
* Temporary Suspension of Membership.
* Suspension of Instructor Approval.
* Suspension of Accreditation.
* Suspension of Professional Certification.
* Revocation of Membership.
* Revocation of Instructor Approval.
* Revocation of Accreditation.
* Revocation of Professional Certification.
* Permanent Ineligibility for specified NIC Nigeria programmes.
* Referral to law enforcement or another competent authority where appropriate.

Sanctions shall be proportionate to the seriousness of the misconduct.

---

# 13. Appeals

Any person or organisation affected by a decision under this Framework may submit an appeal.

Appeals shall be based on one or more of the following grounds:

* Significant procedural error.
* New evidence that was not reasonably available during the original proceedings.
* The decision was unreasonable based on the available evidence.
* The sanction imposed was clearly disproportionate.

Appeals shall be submitted within the timeframe prescribed by NIC Nigeria.

---

# 14. Appeal Panel

Appeals shall be considered by an Appeal Panel independent of the original decision-makers.

The Appeal Panel may:

* Uphold the original decision.
* Modify the decision.
* Reduce or increase the sanction where justified.
* Refer the matter for a new hearing.
* Set aside the original decision.

The decision of the Appeal Panel shall be final within the internal processes of NIC Nigeria.

---

# 15. Records

NIC Nigeria shall maintain secure records of:

* Complaints.
* Investigations.
* Hearing proceedings.
* Decisions.
* Appeals.
* Sanctions.
* Corrective actions.

Records shall be retained in accordance with the Institute's records management requirements.

---

# 16. Confidentiality

All complaints and disciplinary matters shall be handled confidentially.

Information shall only be disclosed where:

* Required by law.
* Necessary to protect the public.
* Required to implement a disciplinary decision.
* Authorised by NIC Nigeria.

Participants in proceedings are expected to maintain confidentiality.

---

# 17. Protection Against Retaliation

No individual shall be subjected to retaliation for making a complaint or participating in an investigation in good faith.

Any attempt to intimidate, threaten, victimise, or disadvantage a complainant, witness, or investigator may constitute a separate disciplinary offence.

---

# 18. Responsibilities

### NIC Nigeria shall:

* Receive complaints.
* Conduct fair investigations.
* Protect the rights of all parties.
* Maintain confidential records.
* Enforce disciplinary decisions.
* Promote accountability and public confidence.

### Members and Accredited Organisations shall:

* Cooperate with investigations.
* Provide truthful information.
* Comply with disciplinary decisions.
* Report serious misconduct where appropriate.
* Respect the integrity of the disciplinary process.

---

# 19. Relationship with External Authorities

Nothing in this Framework prevents matters from being referred to law enforcement agencies, regulatory authorities, professional bodies, or the courts where required by law or in the public interest.

NIC Nigeria may cooperate with external authorities while maintaining its own disciplinary processes.

---

# 20. Review

This Framework shall be reviewed periodically by the Governing Council of the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria) to ensure that complaint handling, disciplinary procedures, and appeal mechanisms remain fair, effective, transparent, and consistent with evolving legal and professional standards.
