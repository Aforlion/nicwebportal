# National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria)

# Regulatory Framework Manual

## Part XIV — Research, Innovation & Publications Framework

**Version:** 1.0

---

# 1. Purpose

The Research, Innovation & Publications Framework establishes the policies governing research activities, innovation, knowledge development, publications, and dissemination of professional information under the authority of the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria).

The Framework recognises that advancing the caregiving profession requires continuous research, evidence generation, innovation, and the sharing of knowledge to improve caregiver education, professional practice, and the quality of care delivered throughout Nigeria.

---

# 2. Objectives

This Framework seeks to:

* Promote research that advances caregiving practice.
* Encourage evidence-based decision-making.
* Support innovation in caregiver education and service delivery.
* Publish professional standards and guidance.
* Contribute to national health and social care policy.
* Encourage collaboration between academia, industry, government, and development partners.
* Establish NIC Nigeria as the national centre of excellence for caregiving knowledge.

---

# 3. Scope

This Framework applies to:

* NIC Nigeria.
* Members of the Institute.
* Accredited Training Partners.
* Accredited Care Facilities.
* Accredited Care Agencies.
* Approved Instructors.
* Researchers working under the authority or sponsorship of NIC Nigeria.

---

# 4. Guiding Principles

Research conducted under the authority of NIC Nigeria shall be:

* Ethical.
* Evidence-based.
* Transparent.
* Independent.
* Respectful of human dignity.
* Scientifically sound.
* Relevant to national needs.
* Conducted in the public interest.

---

# 5. Areas of Research

NIC Nigeria may undertake or support research in areas including:

* Caregiver education.
* Elderly care.
* Home care.
* Disability support.
* Mental health support.
* Dementia care.
* Palliative and end-of-life care.
* Community care.
* Workforce development.
* Caregiver wellbeing.
* Care technologies.
* Digital health.
* Quality improvement.
* Patient and client safety.
* Public health and community outreach.
* Any other area approved by the Governing Council.

---

# 6. Research Ethics

All research undertaken under the authority of NIC Nigeria shall comply with recognised ethical standards.

Researchers shall:

* Respect the rights and dignity of participants.
* Obtain informed consent where required.
* Protect confidential information.
* Maintain data integrity.
* Avoid fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism.
* Declare conflicts of interest.

Where required by law or institutional policy, researchers shall obtain approval from an appropriately recognised Research Ethics Committee before commencing research.

---

# 7. Research Partnerships

NIC Nigeria may establish research collaborations with:

* Universities.
* Teaching hospitals.
* Government ministries, departments, and agencies.
* Professional bodies.
* International organisations.
* Development partners.
* Non-governmental organisations.
* Private sector organisations.

Partnerships shall support the advancement of caregiving knowledge while preserving the independence and integrity of the Institute.

---

# 8. Innovation

NIC Nigeria encourages innovation that improves:

* Caregiver education.
* Professional practice.
* Care delivery.
* Digital learning.
* Assessment methods.
* Quality assurance.
* Workforce development.
* Public awareness.

Innovative projects may be recognised through grants, awards, pilot programmes, or strategic partnerships.

---

# 9. Publications

NIC Nigeria may publish materials including:

* Professional journals.
* Research reports.
* Practice guidelines.
* Curriculum standards.
* Policy papers.
* Technical manuals.
* Position statements.
* Annual reports.
* Books and learning resources.
* Newsletters.
* Statistical reports on the caregiving profession.

Publications shall be reviewed to ensure quality, accuracy, and relevance.

---

# 10. Knowledge Repository

NIC Nigeria shall maintain a digital Knowledge Repository within the NIC Nigeria Portal.

The Repository may contain:

* Research publications.
* Standards.
* Frameworks.
* Practice guidelines.
* Learning resources.
* Policy documents.
* Approved curricula.
* Statistical reports.
* Historical records of the Institute.

Access levels may vary depending on the nature of the material.

---

# 11. Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise agreed in writing:

* Publications produced by NIC Nigeria remain the intellectual property of the Institute.
* Institution-developed curricula remain the intellectual property of the originating institution, subject to the Curriculum Approval Framework.
* Research produced through collaborative projects shall be governed by the applicable partnership agreement.

Proper acknowledgement shall be given to all contributors.

---

# 12. Research Grants and Funding

NIC Nigeria may:

* Award research grants.
* Sponsor research projects.
* Receive research funding.
* Establish research fellowship programmes.
* Partner with funding organisations.

Acceptance of funding shall not compromise the independence, integrity, or objectivity of research findings.

---

# 13. Responsibilities

### NIC Nigeria shall:

* Promote research and innovation.
* Publish professional guidance.
* Maintain the Knowledge Repository.
* Encourage collaboration.
* Protect research integrity.
* Support evidence-based practice.

### Members shall:

* Conduct research ethically.
* Respect intellectual property.
* Share knowledge responsibly.
* Apply evidence to professional practice.

### Accredited Organisations shall:

* Encourage research and innovation.
* Support evidence-based improvements.
* Cooperate with approved research activities where appropriate.

---

# 14. Recognition of Excellence

NIC Nigeria may recognise outstanding contributions to research and innovation through honours such as:

* Research Excellence Award.
* Innovation in Caregiving Award.
* Outstanding Publication Award.
* Distinguished Contribution to Caregiver Education.
* Lifetime Contribution to Caregiving Research.

---

# 15. Relationship with Other Frameworks

This Framework complements:

* The Training & Education Framework.
* The Curriculum Approval Framework.
* The Continuing Professional Development Framework.
* The Professional Certification Framework.
* The Inspection & Compliance Framework.

Research findings may inform future revisions of NIC Nigeria policies, standards, curricula, and professional guidance.

---

# 16. Review

This Framework shall be reviewed periodically by the Governing Council of the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria) to ensure that research, innovation, and publication activities continue to support the advancement of caregiving practice, professional education, and public welfare in Nigeria.
