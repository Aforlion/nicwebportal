# National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria)

# Regulatory Framework Manual

## Part II — Membership Framework

**Version:** 1.0 (Draft)

---

# 1. Purpose

The Membership Framework establishes the requirements, rights, responsibilities, and governance of membership within the National Institute of Caregivers (NIC Nigeria).

Membership provides individuals and organizations with access to the Institute's professional services, education programmes, accreditation systems, certification pathways, and national caregiver registry.

Membership is the foundation upon which all other NIC Nigeria programmes are built.

---

# 2. Objectives

The Membership Framework seeks to:

* Establish a nationally recognized caregiver membership system.
* Promote professionalism within the caregiving sector.
* Maintain an accurate national register of caregivers.
* Support career development.
* Ensure accountability among members.
* Provide access to training, certification, and professional development.
* Strengthen the quality of caregiving services throughout Nigeria.

---

# 3. Membership Categories

NIC Nigeria shall maintain the following categories of membership.

## 3.1 Student Member

Individuals enrolled in caregiver education or training.

Student Members may:

* Access approved training programmes.
* Participate in assessments.
* Apply for internships.
* Build their professional learning record.
* Progress toward NCNA certification.

Student Members are **not** recognised as certified caregivers.

---

## 3.2 Professional Member

Individuals who have satisfied the requirements for professional certification.

Professional Members:

* Hold the NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA) credential or any future NIC professional certification.
* May use approved post-nominal designations authorized by NIC Nigeria.
* May work in accredited facilities and agencies.
* Appear in the National Professional Registry.

---

## 3.3 Instructor Member

Professional Members approved to deliver caregiver education on behalf of NIC Nigeria or an Accredited Training Partner.

Instructor Members must:

* Hold an active professional membership.
* Meet instructor qualification requirements.
* Maintain good standing.
* Complete any mandatory Continuing Professional Development (CPD) requirements once implemented.

---

## 3.4 Institutional Member

Organizations registered with NIC Nigeria.

Institutional Members include:

* Accredited Training Partners
* Accredited Care Facilities
* Accredited Care Agencies

Institutional Membership grants access to organization-specific services but does not replace accreditation requirements.

---

## 3.5 Honorary Member

Individuals recognised by the Governing Council for exceptional contributions to caregiving, education, research, public service, or the advancement of NIC Nigeria.

Honorary Membership carries no regulatory authority unless otherwise approved by the Governing Council.

---

# 4. Membership Registration

Every applicant shall:

* Complete the prescribed registration form.
* Provide valid identification.
* Supply accurate contact information.
* Agree to the NIC Nigeria Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.
* Agree to the NIC Nigeria Regulatory Framework.
* Pay the prescribed membership fee where applicable.

NIC Nigeria reserves the right to verify all submitted information.

---

# 5. NIC Membership Number

Every successful applicant shall receive a unique permanent Membership Number.

Example:

NICN-2026-000001

The Membership Number shall remain with the member throughout their relationship with NIC Nigeria.

It shall appear on:

* Membership profile
* Certificates
* Digital badges
* Professional registry
* Verification records
* Official correspondence

The Membership Number shall never be reassigned.

---

# 6. Rights of Members

Members in good standing are entitled to:

* Access services appropriate to their membership category.
* Participate in approved educational programmes.
* Receive official NIC Nigeria communications.
* Verify their professional credentials through the public registry.
* Apply for certifications and accreditations where eligible.
* Submit complaints and appeals in accordance with NIC Nigeria procedures.

Additional rights may be granted by the Governing Council.

---

# 7. Responsibilities of Members

Every member shall:

* Maintain accurate personal information.
* Uphold the Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct.
* Protect the reputation of NIC Nigeria.
* Provide truthful information in all applications.
* Cooperate during investigations or audits.
* Comply with all applicable policies and standards.

Failure to meet these responsibilities may result in disciplinary action.

---

# 8. Membership Status

Membership status shall be classified as:

* Pending
* Active
* Suspended
* Expired (where applicable)
* Revoked
* Resigned
* Deceased

Only Active Members may access services reserved for members in good standing.

---

# 9. Suspension and Revocation

Membership may be suspended or revoked for reasons including:

* Fraud or misrepresentation.
* Falsification of qualifications or records.
* Serious breach of the Code of Ethics.
* Criminal conduct relevant to professional practice.
* Misuse of NIC Nigeria certificates, logos, or credentials.
* Actions that bring NIC Nigeria into disrepute.

Suspended or revoked members shall lose access to member-only services until their status is restored.

---

# 10. Membership Records

NIC Nigeria shall maintain a secure digital register of all members.

Records shall include:

* Membership Number
* Membership Category
* Registration Date
* Current Status
* Training History
* Certifications
* Digital Badges
* Accreditation history (where applicable)

The register shall support public verification of professional credentials while protecting personal information in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

---

# 11. Membership Progression

Membership categories support clear professional progression.

Student Member
↓

Training Completion

↓

Internship Verification

↓

NIC National Certified Nursing Assistant (NCNA)

↓

Professional Member

↓

Approved Instructor (where eligible)

This pathway encourages lifelong professional development and establishes clear milestones for caregivers within the NIC Nigeria ecosystem.

---

# 12. Review of the Framework

This Membership Framework shall be reviewed periodically by the Governing Council to ensure that it continues to reflect national priorities, international best practices, and the evolving needs of the caregiving profession in Nigeria.

Amendments shall take effect only after formal approval by the Governing Council and publication by NIC Nigeria.
