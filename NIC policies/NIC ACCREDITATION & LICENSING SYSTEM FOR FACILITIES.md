# NIC ACCREDITATION & LICENSING SYSTEM

## FOR CARE FACILITIES IN NIGERIA

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THE SYSTEM

The NIC Accreditation & Licensing System is designed to:

* Ensure **minimum care standards** across facilities
* Protect **vulnerable populations** (elderly, disabled, dependent individuals)
* Standardize **caregiver training and practice**
* Establish **accountability and inspection mechanisms**
* Provide **public trust and credibility**

This system applies to:

* Elderly care homes
* Assisted living facilities
* Disability care centers
* Rehabilitation homes
* Home care agencies

---

# 2. LEVELS OF ACCREDITATION

Facilities are graded based on compliance level.

---

## LEVEL 1: BASIC LICENSE (PROVISIONAL)

**Who this is for:**

* Small care homes
* Startups
* Home-based care providers

**Requirements:**

* Minimum safety compliance
* At least one NIC-certified caregiver (Level 1 or 2)
* Basic documentation system
* Hygiene and sanitation standards

**Validity:**
1 year

---

## LEVEL 2: STANDARD ACCREDITATION

**Who this is for:**

* Established care facilities
* Medium-sized operations

**Requirements:**

* Majority staff NIC-certified (Level 2+)
* Structured care plans
* Incident reporting system
* Basic medical collaboration (doctor/nurse on call)
* Staff training records

**Validity:**
2 years

---

## LEVEL 3: ADVANCED / PREMIUM ACCREDITATION

**Who this is for:**

* High-standard facilities
* Institutional care centers

**Requirements:**

* Certified supervisory staff (Level 3 or 4)
* Full documentation and audit system
* Safeguarding and compliance framework
* Regular staff CPD training
* Emergency preparedness systems

**Validity:**
3 years

---

# 3. CORE ACCREDITATION STANDARDS

Facilities are assessed across **6 key pillars**:

---

## 1. GOVERNANCE & MANAGEMENT

Covers:

* Ownership structure
* Management competence
* Staff supervision
* Policies and procedures

Inspection checks:

* Is there a responsible manager?
* Are roles clearly defined?
* Are staff supervised properly?

---

## 2. STAFFING & TRAINING

Covers:

* Staff qualifications
* NIC certification levels
* Staff-to-client ratio
* Ongoing training

Key rule:

👉 No untrained caregiver should provide unsupervised care.

---

## 3. CARE DELIVERY & PRACTICE

Covers:

* Daily care routines
* Personal care quality
* Nutrition support
* Medication awareness (not administration unless qualified)

Inspectors look for:

* dignity in care
* respect
* proper handling techniques

---

## 4. SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT

Covers:

* fall prevention
* fire safety
* infection control
* building safety

Critical in Nigeria where infrastructure varies widely.

---

## 5. SAFEGUARDING & ETHICS

Covers:

* protection from abuse
* reporting systems
* confidentiality
* respect for rights

Facilities must show:

* clear reporting channels
* no tolerance for abuse

---

## 6. DOCUMENTATION & RECORD KEEPING

Covers:

* care records
* incident reports
* staff logs
* client history

If it is not documented, it is considered **not done**.

---

# 4. ACCREDITATION SCORING SYSTEM

Each facility is scored out of **100 points**.

| Pillar        | Points |
| ------------- | ------ |
| Governance    | 15     |
| Staffing      | 20     |
| Care Practice | 20     |
| Safety        | 20     |
| Safeguarding  | 15     |
| Documentation | 10     |

---

## GRADING

| Score    | Grade | Status                     |
| -------- | ----- | -------------------------- |
| 85–100   | A     | Fully Accredited           |
| 70–84    | B     | Accredited with Conditions |
| 50–69    | C     | Provisional Approval       |
| Below 50 | Fail  | Not Approved               |

---

# 5. INSPECTION SYSTEM

---

## TYPES OF INSPECTIONS

### 1. Initial Inspection

Before licensing

### 2. Routine Inspection

Every 6–12 months

### 3. Unannounced Inspection

Triggered by complaints or risk

---

## WHAT INSPECTORS DO

* observe care delivery
* review records
* interview staff
* inspect environment
* check compliance

---

## INSPECTOR PROFILE

NIC Level 4 certified professionals trained in:

* auditing
* care standards
* safeguarding
* compliance

---

# 6. LICENSING PROCESS

---

## STEP 1: APPLICATION

Facility submits:

* registration details
* staff list
* services offered

---

## STEP 2: DOCUMENT REVIEW

NIC evaluates:

* policies
* staff certification
* care processes

---

## STEP 3: INSPECTION

Physical or virtual inspection conducted.

---

## STEP 4: SCORING & DECISION

Facility receives:

* score
* grade
* improvement recommendations

---

## STEP 5: CERTIFICATION

Facility is issued:

* NIC License Certificate
* Accreditation Level
* Validity period

---

# 7. COMPLIANCE & ENFORCEMENT

NIC may:

* issue warnings
* require corrective action
* suspend accreditation
* revoke license

---

## NON-COMPLIANCE EXAMPLES

* abuse of residents
* untrained staff providing care
* unsafe environment
* falsified records
