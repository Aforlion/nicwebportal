# NIC CAREGIVER EDUCATION SYSTEM

## (LEVEL 1 → LEVEL 4 PROFESSIONAL PATHWAY)

---

# OVERVIEW

NIC will operate a **4-tier structured certification system**, where each level builds competency, responsibility, and earning potential.

Think of it like this:

| Level   | Title                      | Role                    |
| ------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Level 1 | Foundation Caregiver       | Entry-level             |
| Level 2 | Specialized Caregiver      | Skilled practitioner    |
| Level 3 | Advanced Care Practitioner | Clinical-support level  |
| Level 4 | Care Supervisor / Manager  | Leadership & regulation |

---

# LEVEL 1

# NIC CERTIFIED CAREGIVER – FOUNDATION

## Purpose

To produce **safe, entry-level caregivers** who can assist with basic care under guidance.

## Target Audience

* Beginners
* Family caregivers
* Domestic workers transitioning into care
* School leavers

## Core Focus

* Basic care skills
* Hygiene
* communication
* safety awareness

---

## Modules

1. Introduction to Caregiving
2. Personal Hygiene & Infection Control
3. Basic Patient Care (ADLs)
4. Communication Skills
5. Safety & Emergency Awareness
6. Professional Conduct & Ethics

---

## Competencies

* Assist with bathing, feeding, dressing
* Maintain hygiene standards
* Communicate respectfully
* Identify basic health concerns
* Follow instructions safely

---

## Outcome

**Entry-Level Caregiver (Employable in homes)**

---

# LEVEL 2

# NIC CERTIFIED CAREGIVER – SPECIALIZED

## Purpose

To produce **skilled caregivers capable of handling complex care needs independently**.

## Specialization Tracks

Learners choose **one or more**:

1. Elderly Care & Gerontology
2. Disability & Special Needs Care
3. Home Health & Chronic Care
4. Mental Health & Psychosocial Support

---

## Core Focus

* condition-specific care
* risk identification
* independence support
* safeguarding

---

## Competencies

* Manage elderly or disabled clients
* Recognize warning signs
* Support mobility and behavior
* Communicate with families
* Work with minimal supervision

---

## Outcome

**Professional Caregiver (High employability, higher pay)**

---

| ------- | ----- |

| Level 4 | 40–50 |

Annual renewal required.
